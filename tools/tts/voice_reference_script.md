# Voice reference for Chatterbox - what Enes reads (20 Nov 2026)
Record each take as its own WAV (mono, 44.1 or 48 kHz), quiet room, 15-20 cm from the mic, no music. Cut the silence at both ends.
Files stay OUT of git: `voice/enes_ref_<name>.wav` (`voice/` is in .gitignore).

## Take 1 - MAIN reference (narrator, ~25 s) -> voice/enes_ref_main.wav
Calm, a little mysterious, warm. Like telling a friend a strange story late at night. Not reading, telling.

> Why do final bosses always wait in the same room?
> Nobody ever asks them. The hero walks in, the doors slam shut, and the music starts.
> But think about it. Thirty years in one cold castle, guarding one shiny chest.
> Every morning, the same torches. Every night, the same heavy footsteps.
> And yet, when the hero finally arrives... the boss is ready. Every single time.

## Take 2 - CURIOUS / building tension (~10 s) -> voice/enes_ref_curious.wav
> Wait. Something is wrong here. The knight hit him three times, and he got shorter... every time. Why?

## Take 3 - EXCITED / fast (~8 s) -> voice/enes_ref_excited.wav
> He runs! The floor is falling behind him! Five seconds left... four... three!

## Take 4 - DEADPAN / the punchline (~6 s) -> voice/enes_ref_deadpan.wav
> His shield was useless. So he equipped the whole castle.

## Pronunciation notes (only where Turkish habits differ)
- th: "the, think, thirty, three" - tongue between the teeth, not "d"/"t".
- w: "why, walks, wait, warm" - round lips, not "v".
- Final consonants: "hand, cold, ready" - say the last sound clearly.
- Pause at commas and full stops; let the "..." breathe for half a second.
