## Rule of the story
Monsters carry their savings INSIDE their bodies as coins. At midnight the Dark Lord's tax collector takes 100 G from every den;
a den that cannot pay is DELETED. So when you kill a slime, you are taking its family's rent.
Logic check: every slime that dies drops exactly the coins we saw inside it; the den's total is shown and adds up.
