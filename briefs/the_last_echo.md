## Story
Aren, a blue-caped knight, carries a candle that can replay the final memory of a dead person. Each replay burns away the sound of that person's voice from the listener's memory. When the candle goes out, the voice is lost forever.
Aren lights it to hear his dead brother again. In the memory, his brother's three knocks were not a plea for Aren to come back. They were a signal pointing survivors toward an escape tunnel.
In the present, a child trapped in the same ruined watchtower taps the same three knocks. Aren follows the signal, finds the tunnel and brings the child back to the waiting mother. The candle dies. Aren can no longer remember his brother's voice, but he has carried out his brother's last wish.
