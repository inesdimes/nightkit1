# WHY DO MIMICS PRETEND TO BE CHESTS? - brief (21 Nov 2026, Claude) - day 2, 02:00 slot (~30 s), Tone 2.0
Rules: briefs/SERIES_14_DAYS.md ("Tone 2.0"). PS1, nightkit, Enes-timbre voice, calm.

## Story
A treasure chest at the end of a dungeon was opened and emptied by every hero for a hundred years. Nobody ever put anything back.
One day it learned to bite. Now heroes fight it for two seconds and leave. One knight opens it, sees the teeth, does not raise his
sword, takes nothing, and leaves one coin inside.
