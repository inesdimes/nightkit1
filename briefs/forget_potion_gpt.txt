WHAT IF EVERY HEALING POTION MADE SOMEONE FORGET YOU?

Format: 45-second medieval RPG short

0:00–0:03
VISUAL: A wounded adventurer drinks his last potion. The young woman beside him wakes and recoils.
NARRATOR: “What if every healing potion made someone forget you?”
SOUND: Glass bottle clink. Music cuts. A faint heartbeat begins.

0:03–0:06
VISUAL: The woman raises her sword. The adventurer reaches out to her.
WOMAN: “Stay back! Who are you?”
SOUND: Sword drawn.

0:06–0:09
VISUAL: The adventurer stares at a ring on his hand. Inside it is a tiny portrait of the woman.
NARRATOR: “He thought the first loss was just a trick of the light.”
SOUND: The ring turns against his finger.

0:09–0:12
VISUAL: FLASHBACK: During a battle, the adventurer drinks a potion. An old soldier he had rescued thanks him.
NARRATOR: “Then the soldier he’d saved forgot his name.”
SOUND: A brief burst of battle noise.

0:12–0:15
VISUAL: The adventurer enters an inn. The friendly innkeeper now blocks his way, treating him like a stranger.
INNKEEPER: “Rooms are for paying guests.”
NARRATOR: “The innkeeper forgot his face.”
SOUND: Door creak and low tavern chatter.

0:15–0:18
VISUAL: He studies faded writing on the potion bottle: “A LIFE RESTORED. A BOND FORGOTTEN.”
NARRATOR: “The label had been there all along.”
SOUND: A low note as his finger traces the words.

0:18–0:21
VISUAL: At night, beneath an old stone bridge, he finds the woman badly wounded.
NARRATOR: “Then, beneath the old bridge, he found her dying.”
SOUND: Distant thunder and shallow breathing.

0:21–0:24
VISUAL: He looks from the potion to her. His hand hesitates.
NARRATOR: “One potion could save her.”
SOUND: Heartbeat grows louder.

0:24–0:27
VISUAL: He opens the bottle and gives her the potion. Her wound closes. He lowers his head.
NARRATOR: “But it would take the last memory she had of him.”
SOUND: Potion pouring, then silence.

0:27–0:30
VISUAL: The woman opens her eyes. He smiles with relief.
ADVENTURER: “Mira…”
SOUND: Music begins to rise gently.

0:30–0:33
VISUAL: She draws back and looks around, confused.
WOMAN: “I’m sorry… have we met?”
SOUND: Music cuts out.

0:33–0:36
VISUAL: He cannot answer. He places a small wooden horse in her hand.
NARRATOR: “He wanted to tell her everything.”
SOUND: The toy taps softly against her palm.

0:36–0:39
VISUAL: She studies the toy and smiles kindly at the stranger.
NARRATOR: “Instead, he let her believe they’d just met.”
SOUND: Soft wind; birds begin in the distance.

0:39–0:42
VISUAL: He walks away alone. She calls after him.
WOMAN: “Wait! What’s your name?”
SOUND: His footsteps slow.

0:42–0:45
VISUAL: He stops without turning. An RPG notification appears: “QUEST COMPLETE — MEMORY LOST.” Cut to black.
NARRATOR: “He gave her a new life. And kept their story for himself.”
SOUND: One piano note, then silence.
