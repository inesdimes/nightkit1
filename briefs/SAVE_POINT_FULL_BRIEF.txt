3. RULE OF THE STORY
- Every time the hero dies, he wakes up at the same campfire, and the old keeper carves one notch into his ladle.
- Logic check:
  * 0 s: hero wakes up, keeper carving = the rule shown in frame 1.
  * 14 s: the other side of the ladle = the keeper's own deaths from back when he was a hero. Same rule, earlier hero.
  * 30 s: the hero dies off screen (red flash + YOU DIED), and the keeper lifts the knife again = the rule continues.
  * 33 s: the hero wakes up at the fire = frame 1. The loop obeys the rule.

4. CAST
- HERO - HD adventurer (ALREADY BUILT). NO helmet, eyes always visible. Brown leather, short sword, dirty face.
  Lying on a bedroll at the start. Height 1.0 (reference).
- KEEPER - old NPC villager (ALREADY BUILT). Add a short grey beard and a dark green hood (small change).
  Sits on a log by the fire. Seated, he reaches about the hero's shoulder.
  Readable from far away: big wooden LADLE in his left hand, small knife in his right hand.
- PROPS (NEW, cheap):
  * LADLE: long wooden ladle, drawn ~1.5x real size so it reads on a phone.
    Side A: ~99 fresh, light-coloured notches along the handle (wood colour, cut ends brighter).
    Side B: the whole handle covered with dark, faded, grey notches (many more, packed tightly).
  * KNIFE: small and plain.
  * Soup BOWL (wooden), held on the lap only, never at the mouth.
  * Old RUSTY SWORD stuck in the ground behind the keeper (background only, never mentioned by name:
    the viewer connects it at 20 s).
  * Far GATE: dark castle gate silhouette on the horizon with a red glow (castle wall is ALREADY BUILT, reuse it as a silhouette).

5. PLACE
- Night camp (ALREADY BUILT): warm orange campfire, cold blue night around it, a few stars.
  Light carries the story: fire = safe, red gate glow = danger.
- One place only. The gate is just a far background.

8. SOUND
- 0.0: sharp inhale/gasp (hero) + fire crackle bed (whole video).
- 0-28.5: soft 8-bit campfire lullaby, low.
- 5.5-9.5: knife scrape on wood, small wood chip ticks.
- 14.0: wood turning creak; the music dips under line 7.
- 28.5-30.5: music OUT, fire only (the silence is the beat).
- 30.5: far boom + low roar; YOU DIED sting (short, low, retro).
- 31.5-33: no music; a single soft note under "Get up, kid."
- 33.0: save-point chime + white flash; it has to sound like the start of frame 0 (the gasp lands on the rewatch).

9. PLANTED DETAIL / WORDLESS BEAT / LOOP
- Planted: ladle notches, 0.0 s (frame 1) and the 5.5-9.5 s insert. Reversed at 14.0 s: the other side = the keeper's own deaths.
- Wordless: 28.5-30.5 s, the knife stops, no notch: he hopes this one makes it.
- Loop: the last frame (33.5 s) = the frame-0 framing, hero lying just before the gasp; frame 0 starts the gasp + "Again?".

10. MUST / NICE TO HAVE
MUST
- Frame 1: hero close and big, already moving (the gasp), cover text on screen, the ladle visible.
- Hero's eyes visible the whole time (no helmet).
- Bowl stays at lap/hand height, never at the mouth. The knife never goes INTO the ladle (keep a visible gap,
  chips sell the cut). Check hand paths on stills: hand over bowl, turning the ladle, picking up the sword.
- The ladle reads on a phone: oversized, notches high-contrast, side B clearly darker/denser than side A.
- Last frame = frame-0 framing.
- <= 40 s.
NICE
- Firelight flicker on the ladle during the side-B push-in.
- A tiny steam wisp from the bowl.
- The keeper's rusty sword has the same shape as the hero's sword (sharp-eyed viewers spot it).
