## Rule of the story
While the bar loads, a goblin crew builds the next level FOR REAL. The tips are written by the foreman to keep the player reading
(the bar slows down while a tip is on screen). If the bar hits 100 % before they finish, the player walks into an unfinished level.
Logic check: the bar speeds up when no tip is shown and slows when one is; everything the hero sees at the end was built on screen.
