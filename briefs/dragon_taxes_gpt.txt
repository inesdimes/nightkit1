WHAT IF THE DRAGON WAS STEALING THE KING’S TAXES BACK?

Format: 45-second medieval RPG short

0:00–0:03
VISUAL: A knight points his sword at a dragon. The dragon opens its claw; gold coins stamped with the village crest spill onto the stone.
NARRATOR: “What if the dragon’s treasure had never been stolen?”
SOUND: Sword drawn; coins scatter across stone.

0:03–0:06
VISUAL: An old village woman picks up one coin and recognizes the stamp.
WOMAN (shaken, surprised): “That’s our winter tax.”
SOUND: A low musical note.

0:06–0:09
VISUAL: The knight turns toward her without taking his eyes off the dragon.
KNIGHT (stern, suspicious): “The dragon stole it.”
SOUND: The dragon’s heavy breathing.

0:09–0:12
VISUAL: The dragon leans toward the knight but does not attack.
DRAGON (deep, calm): “No. The king did.”
SOUND: The words echo through the cave.

0:12–0:15
VISUAL: The dragon pushes more gold forward with one claw. Every coin bears the same village crest.
NARRATOR: “For years, it had robbed the royal tax wagons.”
SOUND: Coins scrape across stone.

0:15–0:18
VISUAL: The knight lowers his sword slightly. The woman closes her fist around the coin.
WOMAN (angry, quietly): “We starved while the king grew rich.”
SOUND: Wind begins to rise.

0:18–0:21
VISUAL: The knight looks out from the cave. Torches approach along a distant road.
NARRATOR: “Then the king’s soldiers arrived.”
SOUND: Hooves and an approaching horn.

0:21–0:24
VISUAL: An armored captain dismounts and points at the knight.
CAPTAIN (commanding, cold): “Kill the beast. Bring back what belongs to the crown.”
SOUND: Soldiers raise their shields.

0:24–0:27
VISUAL: The knight looks at the dragon, then at the villagers. He slowly lowers his sword.
NARRATOR: “The quest was simple: slay the dragon.”
SOUND: Tension builds in the music.

0:27–0:30
VISUAL: The knight turns his sword toward the captain.
KNIGHT (calm but firm): “I found the real thief.”
SOUND: The sword lifts with a metallic ring.

0:30–0:33
VISUAL: The dragon moves to stand beside the knight. Villagers gather the gold behind them.
DRAGON (low): “Then stand with them.”
SOUND: The dragon spreads its wings.

0:33–0:36
VISUAL: The dragon rises into the sky above the cave. Wind snuffs out the soldiers’ torches.
NARRATOR: “By dawn, the village had its gold back.”
SOUND: Wingbeats and extinguished flames.

0:36–0:39
VISUAL: The woman distributes the gold among the villagers. The knight faces the soldiers at the village gate.
WOMAN (hopeful, determined): “This time, we keep what we earn.”
SOUND: Music shifts to a warm, strong tone.

0:39–0:42
VISUAL: The dragon circles above the village. Its shadow passes over the square.
NARRATOR: “And the dragon kept watch over the road.”
SOUND: Wind and a distant dragon roar.

0:42–0:45
VISUAL: An RPG notification appears: “QUEST UPDATED — PROTECT THE VILLAGE.” The knight lowers his sword and turns toward the villagers.
NARRATOR: “Turns out, the monster had been guarding them all along.”
SOUND: One heroic note, then cut.
