## Rule of the story
The quest marker always points the SHORTEST way, even under a dragon's nest. Mira, the escort, can see the dragon; the hero cannot
(it circles above the clouds). Every "mistake" she makes steers the hero away from the marker's path.
Logic check: each of her detours happens exactly when the dragon's shadow crosses the marker path.

## Stakes + urgency
- Root for: MIRA (farm girl, basket of apples = planted) and the hero.
- Can be lost: her life (ESCORT HP bar), and his.
- Urgency: her HP bar drops with every mistake; wolves; the dragon's shadow sweeping closer.
